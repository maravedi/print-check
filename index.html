How to export from Tinkercad

  1. Press Ctrl + A (or Cmd + A on Mac) to select every shape, then click Group.
  2. Make sure your design rests flat on the workplane (select it and press D).
  3. Click Export in the top-right corner and choose .STL.
  4. Upload that .stl file with the button above (or drag it in), fix any problems, and share the file with your teacher for review.
